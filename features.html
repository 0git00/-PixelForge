PixelForge photo editor features

A practical overview of the tools in the editor, and the limits to know about.

Crop, resize, rotate and flip

Crop freehand or with ratios (1:1, 4:5, 5:4, 16:9, 9:16 or a custom ratio). Resize by pixels or percentage with a locked aspect ratio, or pick a preset such as 1920×1080, 1280×720, 1080×1080 or 1080×1350. Rotate by 90°, 180° or any angle, and flip horizontally or vertically.

Remove objects and retouch

Paint over an unwanted object and PixelForge rebuilds the area from the surrounding pixels. If a removal goes too far, paint over the area and recover it. The Heal/Clone brush copies or blends pixels from a source point you choose, and Edit Text replaces text found in a picture.

Remove or change the background

Click the background to remove it, remove one colour across the whole image, or try auto-detect for plain backgrounds, then refine edges with the erase and restore brushes and a feather control. Replace the background with a solid colour, gradient, preset or your own image. Results depend on how distinct the subject is from the background.

Text, shapes, drawing and layers

Add text with a choice of fonts, colours, outline, shadow and alignment; add rectangles, rounded boxes, circles, lines and arrows; draw with brush, pencil and eraser; and place extra images as layers you can move, resize, rotate, hide, lock and reorder.

Adjustments and filters

Fine-tune brightness, contrast, saturation, exposure, temperature, tint, hue, vibrance, highlights, shadows, sharpness, blur, vignette and more, or start from filters like Vintage, Black & White, Warm, Cool, Cinematic, Dramatic and Retro. Undo and redo are built in.

Export and limits

Open JPG, PNG or WebP files and export as PNG, JPG or WebP with adjustable quality and size. Images larger than 4096 pixels on their longest side are scaled down when opened, and very large exports may exceed what your browser can handle. JPG does not support transparency.
