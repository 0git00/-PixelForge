Privacy Policy

How PixelForge handles images, editing data and information shared through the website.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how PixelForge handles information when you use the PixelForge photo editor and its related informational pages.

Last updated: October 2, 2026

2. Images and editing data

The PixelForge editor states that images are processed locally in your browser whenever possible. The editor does not require an API key or an account.

When an image is loaded into the editor, editing operations are performed in the browser using the page's client-side functionality. Exported files are generated by the browser for you to save.

3. Information we do not intentionally request

The editor interface does not ask you to create an account or provide personal profile information in order to use the core editing workflow.

Do not upload images containing information you are not authorized to process. You remain responsible for the content you choose to edit.

4. Website communications

If you contact PixelForge through a contact method provided on the Contact page, the information you voluntarily send may be used to respond to your message and provide support.

PixelForge should only collect the information reasonably necessary for that communication. Replace the contact details on the Contact page with your actual support address before publishing the site.

5. Cookies and third-party services

The supplied PixelForge editor code does not show an account system or an API integration for its core editing features. This page therefore does not promise that no cookies or third-party services will ever be used on a future version of the website.

If analytics, advertising, embedded services, cloud processing or other third-party technologies are added later, this policy should be updated to describe them accurately.

6. Security and limitations

Browser-based local processing can reduce the need to send image data to a server, but no website or device can guarantee absolute security. Keep your browser and operating system updated and avoid uploading sensitive content to websites you do not trust.

7. Policy changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when PixelForge's features or data practices change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new update date.
